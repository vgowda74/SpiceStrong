## Tips for the reply:
- **Don't mention being fired** — frame it as "after my time at T-Mobile" or "after leaving T-Mobile"
- **Don't ask what the meeting is about** — stay curious but open; let them set the agenda
- **Do mention you built it solo** — this signals both initiative and capability
- **Keep it short** — save the substance for the actual meeting
