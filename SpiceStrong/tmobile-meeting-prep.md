# T-Mobile T-Life Meeting — Preparation Guide

## Your Story (The Narrative Arc)

You were at T-Mobile. You left. Instead of slowing down, you shipped TWO apps to the App Store as a solo founder — SpiceStrong and SpiceChef — built with cutting-edge AI (Claude, DALL-E, ElevenLabs). That's your superpower: you can take an idea from zero to App Store, alone, fast.

Frame it as: "I wanted to build something I was passionate about, and I had the skills to do it end-to-end."

---

## Talking Points by Scenario

### Scenario 1: They Want a Partnership (T-Life Integration)

**What T-Life needs:** Engagement content that keeps customers opening the app. Health, wellness, and lifestyle content is a proven retention driver for super apps.

**Your pitch:**
- SpiceStrong is a ready-made wellness content engine — AI-generated high-protein recipes, personalized to user preferences
- It could integrate into T-Life as a "T-Mobile Tuesdays"-style perk, a wellness section, or a bundled premium feature
- The AI engine means infinite fresh content — not a static recipe database that goes stale
- Privacy-first design (zero data collection) aligns with T-Mobile's Un-carrier brand values

**Key lines to use:**
- "SpiceStrong isn't just a recipe app — it's an AI content engine that generates personalized nutrition content on demand."
- "Imagine T-Life users getting a fresh, personalized high-protein recipe every day, tailored to their dietary preferences — that's a daily reason to open the app."
- "I built this with the same engineering rigor I brought to T-Mobile, but with the speed of a founder who ships fast."

**What to ask them:**
- "What does the T-Life roadmap look like for lifestyle or wellness content?"
- "How do you currently think about third-party integrations vs. building in-house?"
- "What would a pilot look like from your side?"

---

### Scenario 2: They Want to Recruit You

**Your leverage:**
- You've proven you can build full products solo — design, backend, AI integration, App Store deployment
- You understand T-Mobile's ecosystem from the inside
- You have live products generating real user engagement

**Key lines to use:**
- "I've loved building SpiceStrong, but I'm open to conversations about the right opportunity."
- "What I've built in the last few months — two App Store apps with AI, payments, TTS, and full-stack infrastructure — shows what I can do with the right problem."
- "What's the vision for T-Life that you're hiring toward?"

**What to negotiate (if it goes there):**
- Role scope — don't accept less than what your skills warrant (you're a full-stack product builder, not just a coder)
- Ability to keep SpiceStrong on the side (or discuss an acqui-hire)
- Compensation — you have leverage; you're not desperate, you're building

**What to ask them:**
- "What's the biggest technical challenge T-Life is facing right now?"
- "How autonomous are teams on the T-Life side?"
- "What does success look like for this role in the first 6 months?"

---

### Scenario 3: They Want Both (Partnership + Hire)

This is the best outcome. They bring you in to T-Life AND integrate your app.

**Key line:** "I'd be excited to explore how SpiceStrong could become part of the T-Life ecosystem, and I'm open to discussing what that looks like — whether as a partner, a team member, or both."

## Things to AVOID Saying

- Don't say "I was fired" — use "after my time at T-Mobile" or "when I left T-Mobile"
- Don't badmouth T-Mobile or former managers — keep it classy
- Don't undersell yourself — you shipped two apps solo with AI. That's impressive.
- Don't commit to anything in the meeting — "I'd love to think about that and follow up" is always fine
- Don't reveal your financial situation — whether you need income or not, maintain negotiating posture
- Don't give away pricing or licensing terms on the spot — "Let me put together a proposal" buys you time

---

## Power Moves

1. **Show the app live** — have SpiceStrong loaded on your phone, demo-ready. Generate a recipe in real-time.
2. **Know T-Life** — download T-Life before the meeting, explore it, note where wellness/recipe content could fit.
3. **Name-drop the tech** — "Claude AI for generation, ElevenLabs for voice, Supabase for the backend" shows you're current and technical.
4. **Have a one-liner ready** — "SpiceStrong is an AI-powered personal chef for high-protein cooking — it generates recipes, guides you through cooking with voice narration, and tracks your macros."
5. **Follow up within 24 hours** — send a thank-you email with a link to the app and a brief summary of what you discussed.
