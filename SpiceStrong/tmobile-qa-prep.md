# T-Mobile Meeting — Likely Questions & Strong Answers

---

## PARTNERSHIP-TRACK QUESTIONS

### "How many users does SpiceStrong have?"

**If early stage (low numbers):**
"We're early — the app is live on the App Store and growing organically. What's more relevant is that the AI engine works, the product is polished, and it's ready to scale. A partnership like this would be the growth accelerator."

**If you have decent numbers:**
Share them confidently. Monthly active users, recipes generated, retention rates — whatever you have.

**Key:** Don't apologize for being early. Reframe it as an opportunity: T-Mobile gets in early before this takes off.

---

### "What's your business model?"

"Right now SpiceStrong is free to build the user base. The monetization path includes a Pro subscription tier with premium features — advanced meal planning, exclusive recipes, and expanded AI generation. A T-Mobile partnership could be a compelling distribution channel for that premium tier, or we could explore a licensing model where T-Life gets a branded version."

---

### "How does the AI recipe generation actually work?"

"Users describe what they want — protein source, cuisine, dietary preference — and our AI engine (powered by Claude, Anthropic's flagship model) generates a complete, unique recipe with ingredients, step-by-step instructions, and full macro breakdown. It's not pulling from a database; every recipe is generated fresh. We also use ElevenLabs for voice narration so users can cook hands-free."

**Tip:** This is where you demo the app live. Generate a recipe in front of them. The real-time generation is impressive.

---

### "Can this integrate with T-Life technically?"

"Absolutely. The backend is built on Supabase with edge functions, which means we can expose a clean API for recipe generation. Integration could be as lightweight as an embedded webview or as deep as a native SDK. The architecture was built to be modular."

---

### "What would a partnership cost us?"

**Don't throw out a number first.** Instead:

"That depends on the model — content licensing, white-label, customer perk, or API integration all look different. I'd love to understand what T-Life is optimizing for, and then I can put together a proposal that makes sense for both sides."

**If they push:** "I'm happy to scope out 2-3 models with pricing after this meeting once I understand the use case better."

---

### "Would you consider an exclusive deal with T-Mobile?"

"I'm open to exploring exclusivity, especially for a limited period. An exclusive T-Life integration could be a powerful differentiator for both of us. Let's define what that would look like in scope and timeline."

**Key:** Don't give away exclusivity for free. It should come with guaranteed distribution, promotion, or revenue.

---

### "What's on your product roadmap?"

"The next big features are smart meal planning — AI-generated weekly plans based on your goals — community recipe sharing, and grocery list integration with delivery services. For a partnership, I could prioritize features that align with T-Life's wellness strategy."

## RECRUITMENT-TRACK QUESTIONS

### "What have you been up to since leaving T-Mobile?"

"I went all-in on building. I shipped SpiceStrong and SpiceChef to the App Store — both built solo with AI-native architecture using Claude, DALL-E, ElevenLabs, React Native, and Supabase. It's been the most productive stretch of my career."

**Key:** Confident, forward-looking, no negativity about T-Mobile.

---

### "Why did you leave T-Mobile?"

Keep it brief and positive: "It was time for a change, and I wanted to build something of my own. Turns out that was exactly the right move — I've learned more in the last few months shipping products than in years of corporate work."

**If they already know you were let go:** "The transition gave me the push I needed to go all-in on my own products. Sometimes the best things come from unexpected moments."

---

### "Would you consider coming back to T-Mobile?"

"I'm open to the right conversation. What I've built with SpiceStrong shows what I can do when I have ownership of a product end-to-end. If there's a role that lets me bring that same energy — ideally where my app experience and AI expertise are directly relevant — I'd love to hear about it."

**Key:** Don't sound desperate. You have options. You're building.

---

### "What's your experience with [specific technology]?"

Your stack speaks for itself: React Native, TypeScript, Expo, Supabase (PostgreSQL + RLS), Claude API, DALL-E 3, ElevenLabs TTS, file-based routing, edge functions, App Store deployment for iOS and Android. You can speak to all of this with live products as evidence.

---

### "What would it take for you to join our team?"

"I'd want to understand the role, the team, and the vision. Compensation is important, but so is the scope of what I'd be building. I'm also open to creative structures — for instance, if SpiceStrong could be part of the conversation, that could be interesting for both sides."

**Key:** This plants the seed for an acqui-hire or dual arrangement.

## GENERAL QUESTIONS (EITHER TRACK)

### "What makes SpiceStrong different from other recipe apps?"

"Three things. First, every recipe is AI-generated on demand — it's not a static database that runs out of ideas. Second, it's built for fitness-focused users with full macro tracking baked into every recipe. Third, the voice-guided cooking experience makes it genuinely useful while you're cooking, not just for browsing."

---

### "What's been the hardest part of building this?"

"Honestly, doing everything myself — product design, engineering, AI prompt engineering, backend, deployment, App Store optimization. But that's also been the most rewarding part. I understand every layer of this product because I built every layer."

---

### "How do you handle recipe quality and safety?"

"The AI generates within carefully engineered constraints — I built the prompt system to enforce nutritional accuracy, food safety, and dietary compliance. Every recipe includes complete macro breakdowns, and the system respects user-specified allergies and dietary restrictions."

---

### "What's your long-term vision for SpiceStrong?"

"SpiceStrong becomes the default AI-powered nutrition companion for anyone serious about fitness. Not just recipes, but meal planning, grocery ordering, progress tracking, and eventually integration with fitness wearables. The AI engine is the moat — it gets smarter with every interaction."
