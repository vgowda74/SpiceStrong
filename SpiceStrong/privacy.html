Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 26, 2026

SpiceStrong helps you discover, create, and cook high-protein recipes. We keep data collection limited to what is needed to operate the app.

Information We Use

  • Device identifier generated by the app, used for saved recipes, subscription limits, ratings, and meal planning.
  • Recipe inputs, pantry items, nutrition label images, menu images, fridge images, or grocery receipt images that you choose to submit for app features.
  • Purchase and subscription status handled through Apple and RevenueCat.

How Information Is Used

We use information to generate recipes, scan nutrition data, save pantry and meal plan items, restore subscriptions, prevent abuse of free limits, and improve app reliability.

Third-Party Services

SpiceStrong may use services such as Supabase, RevenueCat, Anthropic Claude, OpenAI, fal.ai, and Edamam to provide app features. These services process only the information needed for the selected feature.

Payments

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your full payment card details.

Data Retention

Saved recipes, pantry entries, ratings, and meal plan data may remain until deleted or overwritten. Generated and scanned content may be stored to support app functionality.

Children

SpiceStrong is not intended for children under 13.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@spicestrong.app.
